### Q: What makes a molecule able to cross the blood-brain barrier?

**A:** A molecule can cross the blood-brain barrier (BBB) when its topological polar surface area is below about 90 square angstroms, its molecular weight is under roughly 450 daltons, and its lipophilicity is moderate.

### Q: In one sentence, what is retrieval-augmented generation?

**A:** Retrieval-augmented generation retrieves relevant documents for an answer based on contextual information stored in a knowledge base.
